Date: 15 March 2024

To: Quality Assurance Department
From: MedSupply Europe GmbH, Berlin, Germany
Re: Customer Complaint – Packaging Defect

Dear QA Team,

We are writing to formally raise a quality complaint regarding a recent shipment
of Amoxicillin Trihydrate 500 mg Capsules (USP Grade) received at our Frankfurt
distribution centre on 12 March 2024.

Upon routine incoming goods inspection, our warehouse team identified that
approximately 50 capsules out of a total received quantity of 1,000 units
exhibited visibly broken induction seals on the HDPE bottle closures. The seals
appeared to have never been properly activated rather than tampered with.

Product Details:
  Product Name   : Amoxicillin Trihydrate Capsules
  Strength/Grade : 500 mg / USP
  Batch/Lot No.  : BX-20240315-002
  Mfg. Date      : 15 January 2024
  Expiry Date    : 14 January 2026
  Qty Affected   : 50 units (out of 1,000 units received)

We have quarantined the affected units pending your investigation. The remaining
950 units have been placed on hold and will not be released to market until we
receive your CAPA plan.

Given the potential patient safety implications of compromised packaging, we
request that this matter be treated with HIGH priority and that we receive an
initial acknowledgement within 24 hours.
