  Quantity        : 5,000 tablets (entire batch distributed to 12 retail outlets)

NATURE OF COMPLAINT (Labeling):
During a routine post-market inspection, NMRA field officers identified that
the patient information leaflet (PIL) included in Batch AT-2024-0441-B contains
an incorrect dosage instruction. The PIL states "Take one tablet TWICE daily"
whereas the approved product monograph specifies "Take one tablet ONCE daily."

This labeling discrepancy poses a significant risk of patient overdose if
patients follow the PIL rather than the prescriber's instruction.

REGULATORY CONTEXT:
This constitutes a potential Class II labeling recall trigger under local
medicines regulations. The manufacturer is required to respond within 5 working
days with a corrective action plan and evidence of print-batch reconciliation.

INITIAL ASSESSMENT BY NMRA:
  Severity : Critical (direct patient safety risk — overdose potential)
  Priority : High
